# 🍌 banana

A collection of freely licensed banana images: bananas, plantains, banana plants, flowers, leaves and banana dishes.

- `images/<topic>/` holds the images, sorted into 202 folders such as `banana-bread`, `banana-tree`, `plantains` and `banana-split`, plus folders for banana in other languages (`banane`, `pisang`, `banana-japanese`, `xiangjiao-chinese`, ...). Files are named after their titles, for example `banana-bread/one-bowl-banana-bread-with-cacao-nibs-3.jpg`.
- `CREDITS.csv` lists the title, creator, license and source page for every image.

Off-topic images (things that turned up in searches but aren't bananas) and anything not safe for work were filtered out with an image classifier (CLIP), with the borderline cases checked by hand.

Every image is public domain or under a Creative Commons license. Some licenses are NonCommercial (NC) or NoDerivatives (ND), and most require attribution. Check `CREDITS.csv` before you reuse an image.

Sources: [Openverse](https://openverse.org), [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org), and [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org). The `musa-*` folders hold iNaturalist photos of wild and cultivated banana plants, one folder per species (for example `musa-velutina` is the pink banana). Those photos were resized to 800px.
